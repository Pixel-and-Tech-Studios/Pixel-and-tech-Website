    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 20 November 2026

    Pixel and Tech Studio (“we”, “us”) is based in Newcastle upon Tyne, UK. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and what your rights are. You can contact us at pixelandtech.uk@gmail.com.

    What we collect

    This website has no user accounts, and we don’t ask for personal details to browse it or download our software. We only receive personal information when you choose to send it to us:

    • Contact form: your name, email address, the service you’re interested in and your message.
    • Careers form: your name, email address, what you do and anything you tell us about yourself.
    • Newsletter: your email address.

    These forms open your email app, so the information reaches us as an ordinary email. We receive nothing unless you press send in your email app.

    How we use it

    • To reply to your enquiry and, if you hire us, to carry out the work.
    • To consider you for a role, if you contact us through the careers form.
    • To send you news about our games and software, only if you signed up to the newsletter.

    Our legal basis

    For enquiries, we use your details because you asked us to get in touch and it’s in our legitimate interests to reply. For the newsletter and careers messages, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing us.

    How long we keep it

    We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any project that follows. We keep newsletter addresses until you ask us to stop, and careers details for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

    Who else can see it

    We don’t sell your information. Emails sent to us are handled by our email provider, Google (Gmail). We only share information with people who help us run the business, and only when they need it.

    Cookies and local storage

    We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The Pixel Catch game saves top scores and the name you enter in your own browser’s storage on your device. We never receive that information, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

    Other services

    This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. The site is also hosted by a third party that may keep standard server logs. If a download button takes you to another website, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

    Your rights

    Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

    • tell you what personal information we hold about you,
    • correct anything that’s wrong,
    • delete your information,
    • stop using it, or limit how we use it.

    Email us and we’ll respond as soon as we can. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

    Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
